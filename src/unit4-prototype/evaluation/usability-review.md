# Unit 6 Usability and Clarity Review

The prototype was reviewed with three representative tasks: grant a normal access request, identify why an elevated request is blocked, and identify why an endpoint is noncompliant. Each criterion uses a five-point scale in which 1 means unclear or unusable and 5 means clear and complete for the current prototype.

| Criterion | Score | Evidence and limitation |
|---|---:|---|
| Input clarity | 5 | Every input uses a visible label and a constrained selection list. |
| Navigation consistency | 5 | The three module tabs use the same placement and selected-state behavior. |
| Decision visibility | 5 | Decisions appear immediately with distinct status wording and color. |
| Explanation and remediation | 4 | Failed conditions are listed, but the prototype does not yet provide a guided remediation workflow. |
| Error recovery | 4 | Users can change selections and rerun a decision, but invalid values are prevented by the interface rather than explained in an inline error message. |
| Privacy transparency | 5 | The demonstration notice explains that sample data is used and that no production service is connected. |

The mean score is 4.67 out of 5. The review supports the clarity of the demonstration interface, but it is a single-reviewer heuristic assessment rather than a formal usability study. Future evaluation should include representative users, task-completion time, error frequency, and short post-task feedback.
